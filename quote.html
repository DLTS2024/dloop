Material Guide

🍃 PLA (50-60°C): Environmentally friendly, bio-degradable, economical and practical, suitable for various prototype creations, toys etc.
🎨 PLA Matte (50-60°C): PLA with a smooth, non-glossy finish. Same strength and printability as standard PLA with a refined matte surface ideal for display parts.
🥤 PETG (70-80°C): Strong, tough, and heat-resistant material with excellent clarity, suitable for functional parts, containers, and mechanical components.
🏎️ PETG CF (70-80°C): Carbon fiber reinforced PETG offering high strength, rigidity, and toughness with heat & chemical resistance. Matte finish hides layer lines.
👟 TPU 95A (-40-95°C): Flexible, durable, and elastic material for stretch and impact resistance (phone cases, gaskets, drone parts, robot bumpers).
♻️ rPLA (~50°C): Made from Recycled PLA, ideal for economical prototypes where surface finish and extreme durability are not required.
🛠️ ASA (95-105°C): Strong, durable plastic for functional parts. Offers excellent heat resistance, UV, and impact strength — an upgrade over ABS.
🏎️ Nylon Carbon Fiber (PA12-CF / PA6-CF) (100-150°C): Industrial-grade carbon fiber reinforced nylon with exceptional strength, stiffness, and dimensional stability for automotive, drone frames, and aerospace.
🛠️ Nylon (PA6/66 / PA12) (90-130°C): Tough engineering thermoplastic with high wear resistance, impact resistance, and impact strength.
☁️ PLA Light Weight (FOAM) (50-65°C): ~50% lighter than standard PLA with unique cellular foaming properties. Ideal for RC airplanes & drones.
✈️ ASA Aero (95-105°C): Lightweight foam material, UV resistant with superior temperature & impact resistance compared to PLA Light Weight.
✨ PLA Silk (50-65°C): PLA with luxurious appearance and feel of silk.
🌈 PLA Special / Dual Color: Two-color PLA where printed models have an orientation-based color shift.
🔥 ABS FR / ABS (95-105°C): Flame retardant, strong, durable plastic for electrical enclosures and mechanical prototypes.

Printing Parameters

Layer Height

Determines vertical resolution.
• 0.1mm = Higher quality, longer print time
• Recommended: 0.2mm for most prints
• 0.3mm = Faster printing, draft quality

Infill Density

Determines internal solidity.
• 50-100% = Stronger, more material
• Recommended: 20% for most prints
• 10-20% = Less material, faster

Walls / Shells

Outer layers of the print.
• Recommended: 2-3 walls for standard prints
• 4-5 walls for structural parts

Supports

Structures for overhanging geometry.
• Enable for overhang angles > 45°
• Easily removed post-printing
